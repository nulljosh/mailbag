Iris carries the post

Seven folders

Machine mail goes into a folder. People go to your archive. Your inbox ends up empty.

Seven folders

Shut the box

Receipts, travel, dev, newsletters, social, promotions and junk. Each one is a real label in Gmail or a real folder in iCloud, under a folder called Mailbag.

Sorted twice

Rules first, then a small model

Plain rules know a receipt, a build alert and a boarding pass. A small AI model then reads the sender and subject of machine mail the rules could not place. It never sees mail from people.

People come first

A person is never filed

Mail with no sign of a machine, no unsubscribe link and no no-reply address is treated as a person. Debt and legal notices are never filed either. If something seems missing, look in a folder.

One button

Clear inbox

Files the machine mail and archives the rest. File everything is the gentler one: it leaves mail from people where it is.

Nothing is deleted

Everything has a place

Filing moves mail out of your inbox. Delete is a separate button, and in Gmail it goes to Trash, not the void.

Look first

Preview, then press

The list is a preview. Nothing moves until you press a button. Unsubscribe is one click, sent the way the sender’s own list expects it.

Everywhere you read mail

Same button, every screen

One inbox, three places to press it. Reads happen only when you open the app. No background polling.
